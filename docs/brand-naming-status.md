## Naming Decision

The public project name is:

`Open Industrial Design`

Recommended public presentation:

`Open Industrial Design — Open-source Industrial Design Workspace`

Chinese presentation:

`Open Industrial Design｜开源工业设计工作台`

No separate Chinese brand name is required.

## Why this name

The project is an early-stage personal open-source initiative with limited brand awareness.

A direct descriptive name reduces explanation cost and improves discoverability in:

- GitHub;
- search results;
- social posts;
- community sharing.

## Preliminary collision note

This is a descriptive/open-project style name rather than a highly proprietary invented brand.

That is acceptable for the current project stage.

Before a substantial commercial launch or trademark filing, do a fresh professional review of naming and trademark strategy.
